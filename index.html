What kids learn

Real engineering. Real code. Real confidence.

Your child won't just follow instructions to snap a kit together. They'll think like engineers: invent, test, fail, fix and try again, alongside teammates who become friends.

Science Tech Engineering
Here, they're one.

For far too long, science, technology and engineering have been taught as separate subjects, kept apart by commas and spaces. At Shakti Bots we erase the commas. Engineering is backed by science and powered by technology, and kids experience all three together, every single session.

Design like engineers

Kids learn the "why" behind every mechanism and design their own drivetrains, chassis, arms, harvesters and more.

DrivetrainsChassisArmsHarvesters

Build, test, improve

Ideas become real robots. Kids build them, put them through their paces, find what breaks, and make it better.

PrototypingProblem-solvingIteration

Code with purpose

Kids start with VEX coding blocks and grow into the C programming language, writing code that makes their own robot move.

VEX BlocksC programming

Compete on a real stage

Teams take their robots to 5–6 VEX competitions each season (October–April). Nothing builds poise like a live match.

5–6 events / seasonOct – Apr

Think globally

1–2 online meetups a year with international teams from around the world and real industry experts.

International teamsIndustry experts

Learn from each other

Most importantly, kids teach and learn from one another by explaining ideas, sharing tricks and celebrating each other's wins.
